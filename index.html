Rules

How to Play
Your turn: Play one card from your hand onto an empty cell. Drag it, or tap the card then the cell.
Capture: Compare the touching sides. On a square grid that is top, right, bottom, and left. On a honeycomb, each card also has an upper-right and upper-left number, and you compare whichever sides touch. Strictly higher takes the enemy card.
Ties: If the touching numbers are equal, nothing happens. Equalizer still captures on a tie. In Classic Mode, you can turn on Ties capture in Options so equal numbers capture too. Ember Mode never uses that setting.
Hand: You always hold five cards. On a bigger grid, you draw another card after you play so you stay at five.
How to Win
The grid: When every cell is filled, whoever owns more cards on the grid wins. Cards still in your hand do not count unless Leftover scores is on in Options.
Stats: Numbers run from 1 to 10 (shown as A). Boosts cannot go above 10. Live numbers turn gold when they go up and red when they go down.
The Table
Grid: The usual grid is 3×3, five cards each. In Options you can play 4×4 (eight dealt), 5×5 (thirteen dealt), or a Honeycomb of nineteen cells (ten dealt). You still start with five in hand. Decks use that same deal size. League always uses 3×3.
The deck: The Base Set is 70 cards in fourteen tribes. Every Ember card has an ability. Classic Mode turns abilities off.
Game Modes
Deal Hands: Shuffle the Base Set and deal cards for the grid size in Options.
Draft Hands: Take turns picking cards, back and forth, until both sides have a full deal. Five each on 3×3, eight each on 4×4, thirteen each on 5×5, ten each on Honeycomb.
Decks: Build a deck for the grid in Options and save up to eight per grid. 3×3 is five cards, 4×4 is eight, 5×5 is thirteen, and Honeycomb is ten. Each card costs embers. In a tribe the weakest card is free, then 1, 2, 3, and 4. A whole tribe is 10 embers, so it fits on 3×3. A finished deck plays from the menu. Decks is where you edit the list. Copy the deck code to send that list to a friend, or paste one in to load it. The computer brings a random deck under the same cap. Deal Hands is still a shuffle when you want a fast game.
League: You plus Ash, Vesper, and Rook on 3×3. Draft twenty cards, five each, or bring a finished 3×3 deck. When you bring yours, Ash, Vesper, and Rook each bring a random deck. Then everyone plays everyone twice. Each meeting is best of three. Rankings use series wins, then game wins, then grid score. Leftover scores and Ember/Classic stay locked for the season.
Ember / Classic: Ember Mode uses abilities. Classic Mode is numbers only. Set this in Options before you deal.
Abilities
⚡ Buff: Adjacent allies +1 while this sits.
💀 Curse: On play, the strongest enemy -1, then this card captures.
💥 Blast: +2 to all stats on the play turn.
🦠 Poison: At the end of every turn, neighboring enemies -1, then this card can capture.
⚖️ Equalizer: Captures when touching stats match.
💢 Spite: When this is captured, the captor -1 to all stats.
🔇 Silence: Adjacent enemies lose their abilities. Two Silence next to each other cancel.
🔥 Cinder: On play, a random empty cell becomes Cindered (-1). The cell glows orange.
🔮 Bolt: On play, a random enemy -2, then this card captures.
❄️ Chill: Adjacent enemies -1 while this sits. Counts for captures.
🪞 Parasite: On play, copies an adjacent enemy's ability. If that ability fires on play, it fires before this card captures.
🌀 Siphon: On play, steals 1 from each adjacent enemy's touching number onto the opposite side of this card.
⏳ Pendulum: At the start of every turn, swaps top and bottom with left and right.
🌱 Symbiosis: +1 to all stats for each capture you have made. You see it in your hand, and it counts on the turn you play the card. After that fight, the card goes back to its printed numbers.
