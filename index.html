Rules

The table
The deck: The Base Set is 50 cards in ten tribes. Every Ember card has an ability. Shuffle it and deal two hands of five, or spread sixteen and draft until each hand is five.
League: You plus three named AI. Snake-draft twenty cards (five each), then a six-week double round-robin. You play each rival twice. Each meeting is best of three, and first player swaps so both get an opener. Their series resolve with the same engine and land on the table. Standings break ties by series wins, then game wins, then combined board score. Leftover and Ember/Classic lock at season start. All three AI use the Options difficulty. Backing out saves the season.
Tribe Decks: Each deck is one tribe of five. Pick yours and the opponent's, or leave either one random. Same tribe on both sides is a mirror match.
Placement: On your turn, play one card from your hand onto an empty cell of the 3x3 grid.
Capturing: When your card touches an enemy card, compare touching values (Top, Right, Bottom, Left). If your attacking stat is strictly greater, you capture their card!
Victory: Two hands of five. After the board fills, most cards on the board wins. Leftover in hand does not count. Options can turn leftover scores on, which makes a 5-4 board a 5-5 draw.
Stat Cap: Stats range from 1 to 10 (represented by A). Stat boosts cannot raise a value above 10.
Ember Mode Abilities
⚡ Buff: While this card sits there, adjacent friendly cards get +1 to all stats. Radiance tribe - Celestial, Fairy, Unicorn, Paladin, Angel.
💀 Curse: On play, the strongest enemy loses 1 from all stats, then this card captures. Grave tribe - Ghost, Vampire, Skull, Zombie, Bat.
💥 Blast: +2 to all stats on the turn this card is played. Beasts tribe - Dragon, T-Rex, Lion, Shark, Eagle.
🦠 Poison: At the end of every turn, adjacent enemies lose 1 from all stats. Does not capture on the tick - later plays use the lower stats. Vermin tribe - Spider, Scorpion, Snake, Frog, Beetle.
⚖️ Equalizer: Captures when touching stats match. Balance tribe - Monk, Titan, Robot, Owl, Alien.
💢 Spite: When this card is captured, the captor loses 1 from all stats. Wrath tribe - Cyclops, Gorilla, Wrath, Vendetta, Oni.
🔇 Silence: Adjacent enemies lose their abilities while this card sits there. Capture still works. Next to an enemy Silence, both cancel - neither silences anyone else. Hush tribe - Dread, Hush, Raven, Ninja, Cat.
🔥 Cinder: On play, a random empty cell becomes Cindered. Whoever sits there is -1 to all stats. The cell glows orange. Ember tribe - Scorch, Brand, Fox, Phoenix, Lizard.
🔮 Bolt: On play, zaps a random enemy by -2 to all stats before this card captures. The zap can help this play steal that card. Arcane tribe - Wizard, Genie, Witch, Mage, Imp.
❄️ Chill: While this card sits there, adjacent enemies are -1 to all stats. Counts for captures. Frost tribe - Frost, Wolf, Penguin, Polar, Seal.
