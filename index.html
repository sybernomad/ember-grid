🔥 EMBER GRID 🔥

A tactical card battle game! Place cards on the 3×3 grid, out-stat adjacent sides to flip enemy cards, and control the majority of the board to win.

Quick Rules:
• Stats: Top, Right, Bottom, Left (1-10 / A).
• Capturing: Play next to an enemy; if your touching stat is higher, you flip their card!
• Abilities: Special icons (⚡ Buff, 💀 Curse) passively alter adjacent card stats while active on the board.
AI Difficulty:

HOW TO PLAY

1. Card Stats: Each card has 4 numbers (Top, Right, Bottom, Left) ranging from 1 to 10 (displayed as Aces/A).

2. Placing & Deselecting: Click a card in your hand to select it, then click an empty cell. Click your selected card again to deselect.

3. Abilities & Auras: Cards with abilities (⚡ Buff, 💀 Curse, 🔮 Hex) passively boost or weaken adjacent/target cards as long as they stay on the board!

4. Winning: The player controlling the majority of the board when all 9 squares are filled wins the match!
