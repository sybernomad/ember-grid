🔥 EMBER GRID 🔥

Core Rules
⚔️ Capture: Play next to an enemy card. If your touching stat is higher than theirs, you capture it.
🏆 Victory: Fill the 3x3 grid. The player controlling the most cards at the end wins!
Ruleset Options
Game Mode:
Ember Mode: Cards possess special tactical abilities (Buffs, Curses, Bolts, Blasts, etc.) that interact across the grid.
AI Difficulty:

RULES & COMPENDIUM

Basic Mechanics
Placement: Players take turns placing one card from their hand onto an empty slot on the 3x3 grid.
Capturing: When your card touches an enemy card, compare touching values (Top, Right, Bottom, Left). If your attacking stat is strictly greater, you capture their card!
Stat Cap: Stats range from 1 to 10 (represented by **A**). Stat boosts cannot raise a value above 10.
Ember Mode Abilities
⚡ Buff: Boosts adjacent friendly cards by +1 stat.
💀 Curse: Reduces the strongest enemy card's stats by 1 on play.
🔮 Bolt: Zaps a random enemy card on the board, reducing its stats by 2 on play.
⚖️ Equalizer: Captures if touching stats match exactly. (Monk, Titan)
💥 Blast: Temporarily grants +2 to all stats on the play turn.
🦠 Poison: Reduces all adjacent cards' stats by 1 at turn end.
