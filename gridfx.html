Rules

How to Play
Your turn: Play one card from your hand onto an empty cell. Drag it, or tap the card then the cell.
Capture: Compare the touching sides (top, right, bottom, left). Strictly higher takes the enemy card.
Ties: If the touching numbers are equal, nothing happens. Equalizer still captures on a tie. In Classic Mode, you can turn on Ties capture in Options so equal numbers capture too. Ember Mode never uses that setting.
Hand: You always hold five cards. On a bigger grid, you draw another card after you play so you stay at five.
How to Win
The grid: When every cell is filled, whoever owns more cards on the grid wins. Cards still in your hand do not count unless Leftover scores is on in Options.
Stats: Numbers run from 1 to 10 (shown as A). Boosts cannot go above 10. Live numbers turn gold when they go up and red when they go down.
The Table
Grid: The usual grid is 3×3, five cards each. In Options you can play 4×4 (eight cards dealt) or 5×5 (thirteen dealt). You still start with five in hand. League and Tribe Decks always use 3×3.
The deck: The Base Set is 50 cards in ten tribes. Every Ember card has an ability. Classic Mode turns abilities off.
Game Modes
Deal Hands: Shuffle the Base Set and deal cards for the grid size in Options.
Draft Hands: Take turns picking cards, back and forth, until both sides have a full deal. Five each on 3×3, eight each on 4×4, thirteen each on 5×5.
Tribe Decks: Each side gets one tribe of five on 3×3. Pick both decks, or leave either one random. Same tribe is a mirror match.
League: You plus Ash, Vesper, and Rook. Draft twenty cards (five each), then play a season on 3×3 where everyone plays everyone twice. Each meeting is best of three. Rankings use series wins, then game wins, then grid score. Leftover scores and Ember/Classic stay locked for the season.
Ember / Classic: Ember Mode uses abilities. Classic Mode is numbers only. Set this in Options before you deal.
Abilities
⚡ Buff: Adjacent allies +1 while this sits.
💀 Curse: On play, the strongest enemy -1, then this card captures.
💥 Blast: +2 to all stats on the play turn.
🦠 Poison: At the end of every turn, neighboring enemies -1. Poison does not capture; later plays use the lower numbers.
⚖️ Equalizer: Captures when touching stats match.
💢 Spite: When this is captured, the captor -1 to all stats.
🔇 Silence: Adjacent enemies lose their abilities. Two Silence next to each other cancel.
🔥 Cinder: On play, a random empty cell becomes Cindered (-1). The cell glows orange.
🔮 Bolt: On play, a random enemy -2, then this card captures.
❄️ Chill: Adjacent enemies -1 while this sits. Counts for captures.
